Privacy Policy — Diario Scuola Plus

Diario Scuola Plus (also listed as School Diary Plus, Schulplaner Plus, Agenda Scolaire Plus and other localized names) · Last updated: September 30, 2026 · Contact: konechocoproduction@gmail.com

1. Your school data stays on your device

Everything you enter — profile (name, school, class), timetable, subjects, grades, homework, exams, absences, notes, photos, flashcards, goals, teachers' contacts and audio recordings of classes — is stored only on your device. There is no account and no server: we never receive this data. The study plan and all calculations (averages, "what grade do I need", flashcard scheduling) run entirely on the device.

2. Permissions

  • Microphone — only to record classes, when you start a recording.
  • Camera and photos — only to attach photos (for example of the board) to homework and notes. Photos are saved in the app's private storage.
  • Notifications — reminders for homework and exams, and the end of a Pomodoro, scheduled on the device.

3. Backup and calendar export

You can export a backup file (JSON) or your upcoming homework and exams as a calendar file (.ics) through the system share sheet. Where you save or send these files is your choice.

4. Advertising

The app is free and shows non-personalized ads: full-screen videos from Unity Ads (Unity Technologies) when the app starts and at most every five minutes, and a banner from Google AdMob. The app does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. In the EEA and UK we ask for consent with Google's certified consent form; you can change your choice in Settings → Ad privacy choices. These SDKs may process device type, operating system, language and country, approximate location derived from the IP address, ad views and clicks and diagnostic data. They never access your school data.

  • Unity Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data in ads

5. "Remove ads" purchase

A one-time in-app purchase removes all ads. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google: we never receive payment details.

6. Children

The app is designed for students. Ads are non-personalized and no personal data is sent to us. We recommend parental supervision for users under 13.

7. Your rights

Since we don't hold your school data, you control it: you can edit or delete it at any time, and deleting the app deletes everything. For questions about advertising data write to us or use the links above.

8. Changes

We will update this page if the app's data practices change. The date at the top is the current version.

Informativa sulla privacy — Diario Scuola Plus

Ultimo aggiornamento: 30 settembre 2026 · Contatto: konechocoproduction@gmail.com

I tuoi dati restano sul dispositivo. Profilo, orario, materie, voti, compiti, esami, assenze, appunti, foto, flashcard, obiettivi, contatti degli insegnanti e registrazioni audio sono salvati solo sul tuo dispositivo: nessun account, nessun server. Piano di studio e calcoli (medie, "che voto mi serve", ripasso delle flashcard) avvengono sul dispositivo.

Permessi. Microfono solo per registrare le lezioni; fotocamera e foto solo per allegare immagini a compiti e appunti; notifiche per i promemoria.

Backup ed esportazione calendario. Puoi esportare un file di backup (JSON) o un file calendario (.ics) tramite il menu di condivisione del sistema.

Pubblicità. Annunci non personalizzati: video a schermo intero di Unity Ads all'avvio e al massimo ogni cinque minuti, e un banner di Google AdMob. Nessun tracciamento tra app e siti di altre aziende. In SEE e Regno Unito chiediamo il consenso con il modulo certificato di Google (modificabile in Impostazioni). Gli SDK pubblicitari non accedono mai ai tuoi dati scolastici.

Acquisto "Rimuovi la pubblicità". Acquisto unico gestito da Apple o Google: non riceviamo dati di pagamento.

Minori. App pensata per studenti; consigliamo la supervisione dei genitori sotto i 13 anni.
