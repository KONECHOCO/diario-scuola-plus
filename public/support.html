Frequently asked questions

How do I change the grading scale? Settings → School and grades. The app supports 1–10, 0–20, German 1–6 (1 = best), 1–6, 1–5, 1–12, percentages, US letter grades (A–F with GPA) and Swedish A–F. Grades you already entered are converted automatically.

My school has a different timetable every other week. Turn on "Alternating weeks timetable (A/B)" in Settings and choose whether the current week is A or B. Each class can be set for every week, week A only or week B only.

What does "What grade do I need?" do? In Grades, pick a subject and a target average: the app calculates the minimum grade you need on the next test, taking weights into account.

How do I move my data to a new phone? Settings → Backup → Export backup, save the file (Files, Drive, email…), then Import backup on the new phone.

Can I import flashcards from Anki or Quizlet? Yes: Flashcards → Import, then paste the text or choose the exported .txt/.csv file (question and answer separated by a tab, semicolon or comma).

How do I remove ads? Home or Settings → Remove ads (one-time purchase). Already bought it? Tap "Restore purchases".

How do I add homework and exams to my phone calendar? Calendar → Export to calendar, then open the .ics file with your calendar app.
