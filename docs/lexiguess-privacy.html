Privacy Policy for LexiGuess Word Game

Last updated: August 11, 2026

LexiGuess Word Game is a word puzzle app published by Ikonet Solutions.

Data stored on your device

The app stores gameplay preferences, tutorial state, statistics, and progress locally on your device. This local data is used to provide the game experience and is not used to identify you by Ikonet Solutions.

Advertising

The free version of the app uses Google AdMob to display ads. Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect data such as device identifiers, advertising data, product interaction data, IP address, diagnostics, and performance data. This data may be used by Google and its partners for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and service improvement.

For more information about Google's advertising data practices, visit Google Advertising Policies.

Definitions and third-party content

When you request a word definition, the app may make an HTTPS request to public dictionary services such as Wiktionary. These services may receive technical information such as your IP address as part of normal web request handling.

Purchases and accounts

The app does not require account registration. The app does not require a login or demo account for review.

Contact
